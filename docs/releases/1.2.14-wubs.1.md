# Waypoint 1.2.14-wubs.1

Fixes the installed-only reorder boundary. Available Games, its header and pending-install rows cannot start a table drag. Drops into the available section, onto its header, outside valid bounds or from unrelated/stale sources are rejected. A gap immediately after the last installed game remains valid. The model also rejects noninstalled source/target identities.

After a drag ends or is canceled, row views refresh after AppKit cleanup so a rejected gesture cannot leave a source row blank. Drag eligibility still comes from the cached scan/row state, with no drive probes during dragging.

Existing installed-game ordering and saved positions are preserved. No game content, authentication, launch-only update cadence or bundle identity changes. Apple silicon only; ad-hoc signed, not notarized.
