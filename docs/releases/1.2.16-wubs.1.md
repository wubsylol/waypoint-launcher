# Waypoint 1.2.16-wubs.1

Choose a portrait for each saved account through Account → Choose Portrait…. The compact picker offers four World of Warcraft race portraits and seven Diablo III class portraits, downloaded directly from Blizzard's CDN and cached for offline use. The Waypoint tile restores the circular application icon. Each account remembers its own choice without another sign-in.

Existing accounts and game selections are preserved. Missing images fall back to the Waypoint icon. These are curated game portraits, not synchronization with the avatar selected in Battle.net.

Apple silicon only; ad-hoc signed, not notarized. Bundle identity remains io.wubs.waypoint-review. No self-update feed is enabled.
